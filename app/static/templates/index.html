A BUILDING BLOCK FOR OPEN SCIENCE

So, what is a Nanopublication?

A Nanopublication is a small, self-contained knowledge graph of a single assertion, packaged with information about its provenance and publication context. Nanopublications are already used to register biodiversity statements or ontology terms.

Think of it as a precise, citable knowledge unit: not just what is claimed, but also where the claim came from and when it was made.
