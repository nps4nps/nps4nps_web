Getting started with Nanopublications.

Learn how to contribute structured knowledge about natural products. Find out how templates work, set up your account, and explore the available nanopublication templates.

01 / THE CONCEPT

From structured input to knowledge graph.

Nanopublication templates provide a structured way to contribute scientific information. Each template defines which information should be provided, how individual fields relate to each other, and how the submitted data is represented.

Instead of entering information in an unstructured text format, users fill in predefined fields. By using ontologies, this helps ensure consistent data input and makes the resulting information easier to validate, compare, and reuse.

Once submitted, the information is transformed into the corresponding graph representation and stored as a nanopublication in the federated Nanopublications knowledge graph. Its assertion captures the scientific statement, while provenance and publication information document its origin and authorship.

In this way, individual contributions become connected pieces of structured knowledge that can be queried, linked, and reused.

02 / GETTING STARTED

Three steps to start contributing.

Before creating nanopublications, you need an account and approval to contribute to the project.

STEP 01

Have an ORCID account

To create Nanopublications, you need an ORCID account. ORCID provides a persistent researcher identifier that allows your contributions to be associated with your academic identity.

Create an ORCID account
STEP 02

Create your introductory nanopublication

Head over to Nanodash and sign in with your ORCID account. Then, go to your profile page and create your introductory nanopublication. This initial contribution establishes your presence and provides the information required for the approval process.

Explore the templates
STEP 03

Get approved

To become an approved contributor, your introductory nanopublication needs to be reviewed and approved by an existing authorized user.

If you need assistance with the approval process, please get in touch with the project team.

Contact the project team
